### **How long will I need to stay in Antalya for a tummy tuck?**

We recommend a stay of at least 9-10 nights. This allows for the pre-operative consultation, the surgery, initial hospital recovery, and crucial post-operative check-ups before you are cleared to fly home.

### **Is it safe to travel to Turkey for cosmetic plastic surgery?**

Absolutely. Turkey has a robust and highly regulated healthcare system, especially in private clinics catering to international patients. Mert Meral Aesthetic Clinic adheres to stringent safety and hygiene protocols that meet or exceed international standards.

### **What is the recovery time for an Abdominoplasty?**

Initial recovery, where you need to rest and avoid strenuous activity, is about 2 weeks. You can typically return to a desk job after this period. Full recovery, including the resumption of all exercise and heavy lifting, can take 6-8 weeks. Your final, refined results will continue to improve over several months.

### **Will my Abdominoplasty results be permanent?**

The results of a tummy tuck are designed to be long-lasting. The excess skin and fat cells that are removed are gone permanently. However, future significant weight fluctuations or pregnancies can alter the results. Maintaining a stable weight is key to preserving your new contour.

### **Can I combine an Abdominoplasty with other procedures?**

Yes, it is very common to combine an Abdominoplasty with other procedures like liposuction (to further contour the waist and flanks) or breast surgery. This is often referred to as a "Mommy Makeover." Discuss your goals with your surgeon to see if a combined procedure is right for you.

### **What kind of scar should I expect from a tummy tuck?**

You should expect a horizontal scar located in the lower abdomen, typically running from hip to hip. Surgeons at Mert Meral Aesthetic Clinic take great care to place the incision low enough to be easily hidden by most underwear and bikini bottoms. The scar will fade significantly over time.

### **How much pain is involved with an Abdominoplasty?**

Patients will experience discomfort and soreness, particularly in the first few days post-surgery, which is managed effectively with prescribed pain medication. The level of pain is generally described as manageable and subsides progressively each day.

### **Who is the ideal candidate for an Abdominoplasty in Turkey?**

The ideal candidate is a non-smoker in good general health, at a stable weight, who is bothered by the appearance of excess skin and fat in their abdominal area. It is important to have realistic expectations about the outcome of the surgery.
