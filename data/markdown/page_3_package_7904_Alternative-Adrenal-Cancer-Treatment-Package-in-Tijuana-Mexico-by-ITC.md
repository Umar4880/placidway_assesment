## A Comprehensive Guide to Your Rhytidectomy (Face Lift) Procedure in Seoul

A rhytidectomy, commonly known as a facelift, is a cosmetic surgical procedure designed to create a more youthful facial appearance. Its primary purpose is to address visible signs of aging, such as deep creases below the lower eyelids, sagging in the mid-face, lines around the nose and mouth (nasolabial folds), and the development of jowls by tightening underlying muscles and redraping the skin of your face and neck.

The procedure works by making incisions discreetly along the hairline and around the ear. The surgeon then lifts and repositions the deeper facial tissues (SMAS layer) to restore a more youthful contour, removes excess skin, and closes the incisions. The benefits are significant, leading to smoother skin, a more defined jawline, and a reduction in wrinkles and sagging. Recovery typically involves 1-2 weeks of initial downtime for swelling and bruising to subside, with final, settled results visible after 2-3 months. Success rates are very high, with most patients reporting immense satisfaction with their natural and long-lasting results.
